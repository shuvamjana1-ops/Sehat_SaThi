Between clinic visits

The hard part happens between doctor visits.

A follow-up visit often lasts two minutes. When you get home, you are holding a handwritten slip, three tablet strips, and a blood sugar meter.

When a doctor switches brands, it is easy to take two tablets that contain the exact same active salt. When sugar drops at 2 AM, writing numbers in a notebook does not wake up your family.

Running out of tablets on a Sunday evening or paying more when an exact-strength generic exists adds quiet daily stress.

"If blood sugar drops below 54 mg/dL, show Call 112 and Call family right away without scrolling."

Honest limits

What SehatSaathi cannot do.

  • Not a doctor

    It never diagnoses an illness or replaces emergency medical services.

  • Does not change or stop medicines

    Dose changes always stay with your doctor or pharmacist.

  • Alerts open your messaging app and you tap Send

    WhatsApp, SMS, and phone calls are pre-filled on your phone so no paid background server is required.

  • Sample medicine data needs verification

    The bundled 77 medicines and 30 interaction pairs are sample data; reminders also may not appear when a browser tab is closed.
