Chronic Care & Medication Companion

Calm, clear daily support for blood sugar, blood pressure, and medicines.

SehatSaathi helps older adults and their families log sugar and blood pressure readings, check medicine interactions and lower-cost generic equivalents, track tablet refills, and manage private caregiver sharing.
