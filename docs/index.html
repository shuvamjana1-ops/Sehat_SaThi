How SehatSaathi helps after the clinic visit

Tap any card below to jump straight to that screen in the preview:

1. Read your prescription

Take a photo of your prescription. We read it, you check it. Duplicate salts and Indian generic options are flagged from our 100-medicine list.

2. Log sugar and BP

If your sugar drops below 70 mg/dL, we show you what to do first using fixed clinical rules rather than AI guesses.

3. Track daily doses

Mark Morning, Afternoon, or Night medicines as taken, listen aloud in Indian English, and watch stock counts update.

4. Refills and Care Card

2 tablets left is about 2 days. See refill warnings early, edit patient details, and print the 1-page Doctor Summary.

What SehatSaathi cannot do

  • It is not a doctor and cannot diagnose any illness.
  • It never tells you to start, stop, or change a medicine dose.
  • It checks duplicate salts, interactions, and generic options only against its bundled 100-medicine reference list. Anything outside that list must be verified by your pharmacist.
  • Reading a new prescription photo requires internet, though keypad logging and your schedule work offline on your phone.
